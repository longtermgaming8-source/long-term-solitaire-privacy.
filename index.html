Long Term Solitaire Privacy Policy

Long Term Gaming
Effective date: October 1, 2026
Android package: com.longtermgaming.solitaire

This policy explains how Long Term Solitaire handles information. For privacy questions, contact longtermgaming8@gmail.com.

Game data stored on your device

The game stores unfinished games, card layouts, undo history, elapsed time, moves, wins, daily challenge records, collection progress, and settings in private app storage on your device. These records support saving and resuming games, displaying records, and remembering your preferences. The game does not require an account and does not upload these gameplay records to a Long Term Gaming server.

Android backup

The app permits Android backup. Depending on your device and Google account settings, Android may back up or transfer app data, including saved games and preferences. You can manage backup through your device settings. Google handles its backup services under its own privacy policy.

Advertising and Google services

The current release configuration disables banner advertising and in-app purchases. Google Mobile Ads, Google's User Messaging Platform, and Google Play Billing libraries are included in the app. Disabling the game's advertising feature is not a guarantee that bundled platform services perform no processing.

When advertising is enabled in a release, Google Mobile Ads may collect and share IP addresses, which can indicate approximate location; device or other identifiers, including advertising identifiers; advertising interactions; and diagnostic information. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Advertising and consent processing depend on the release configuration, your region, and your privacy choices. Where required, the app's consent flow and Privacy choices control provide available choices.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures. Your device may also provide controls to reset or delete its advertising ID.

Purchases

If ad-removal purchases are enabled in a release, Google Play processes payment. The app receives purchase information, such as the product identifier, purchase status, token, and signature, to verify and restore the purchase. It stores ad-removal status locally. Long Term Gaming does not receive your payment-card details through the app. Removing ads does not delete Google's transaction records.

Information you send to us

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to support and privacy requests. Email is processed by our email provider. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed for your request.

Sharing and security

The app does not include a developer-operated account system, public chat, or developer analytics service. Information handled by Google services is subject to those services' practices. Support messages may be processed by our email provider. We may disclose information when required by law or to address fraud or security issues. Private Android app storage helps protect local records; no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until they are overwritten or removed. You can remove local game data through Android Settings → Apps → Long Term Solitaire → Storage → Clear storage. This removes your local progress and settings. Uninstalling also removes local app storage, but Android backups may remain or restore data later. Manage backups separately in your device or Google account settings.

You can email us to request deletion of support correspondence. We retain support information only as needed to handle requests and meet applicable legal obligations. Google controls retention and deletion of information processed through its services; use the relevant Google account and privacy controls. There is no Long Term Solitaire account to delete.

Children's privacy

The app does not request a child's name, age, contact details, or other personal information to play. If you believe a child has sent us personal information in a support message, contact us so we can address the request.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the app's information practices change, including when advertising or purchases are activated, and revise the effective date.
